2. Problem

An agent that can diffuse sabotage will beat any monitor that checks individual actions. There have been attempts at using AI agents to help with some tasks in AI research. As long as that agent isn't aligned with you they can just do nothing that stands out. It will be most cost effective for an agent to sabotage many things slightly. Terekhov et al ("Diffuse AI Control on Fuzzy Tasks" arXiv 2606.08892) described this as a control problem. They propose solving it through training as monitoring and incriminating seem "theoretically and empirically less promising for mitigating diffuse threats" (p.2). You can compare an action to a threshold of what an honest agent would do. If it's not above that you have to build up evidence. This could apply to many agents. Attacks that happen across multiple agents, sessions or PRs can also avoid being detected by monitors that check one thing at a time (arXiv 2607.07368, 2605.31593, 2607.02514). A single monitor that watches all actions will be costly. You can attack the monitor. Agents can interact with your monitor. Monitors that oversee AI: Can be swayed by rhetoric (arXiv 2505.23575); Will take an attacker's word for their score (arXiv 2510.09462); Can be talked into approving by the agent we can quantify this with approval rate (arXiv 2607.08066). It is easy to evade activation probes if you can modify the weights (arXiv 2609.36490). They found that you could not evade by shifting enough given only context. To our knowledge, no one has investigated how much it takes to shift a probe as a function of type of attack and strength. Nor compared it to shifting a judge.

Why white box.

Developer will have access to activations if they are using agents in house. We can build a case if there is some internal indication that an agent is trying to sabotage. Maybe not externally. Not sure if this will be possible with model organisms we can expect to exist. Will find out through this project and can pre commit to our next steps.

3. Theory: A Lemma, Negative Theorem and Important Question

Let Xi,t be the monitor score for agent i ∈ {1,...,N} and step t ∈ {1,...,T}. Time (N=1) and Swarm (N=k) are equivalent. Both can be thought of as subgroups of the permutations over the index set. Permuting time for an agent and agents for a step. So we can have a single theory for paper A and paper B.

Lemma (L) Additivity if attacker exists.

Attacker has budget B = Σδk to shift means of agents, steps, at what point, how much and where. Can do so adaptively. Defender has an anytime valid bound on false positives.

Terminal invariance.

Sum at the end will be changed by B regardless of how you distribute your budget. So its power can only be a function of B, horizon n0 = NT, distribution of honest scores and alpha. Will decrease as n0 grows given B.

Max comparison.

If you have a cutoff exactly then your max over actions will become less powerful the more you distribute your attack.

Anytime valid.

We want the attacker to sabotage early. There is a lower bound on power as a function of just (B,n0) for any schedule. Can calculate the cost of waiting and being anytime valid.

Detect before harm.

If we want to say that we are secure if we can detect before it causes θ damage. Then we need something more than a cumulative e process. Need a change detection process (Page's CUSUM, e detectors). Two things to note. Power is not an optimal metric to use with optional stopping. We want to maximize expected log growth in the worst case (GROW) (Pérez-Ortiz, Lardy, de Heide and Grünwald arXiv 2208.07610). And that there is a budget is something we are assuming. Nearest paper to having a hidden adversary (Ramtin, Nain and Towsley arXiv 2601.20022) shows that you get Θ(γ) delay compared to log γ. They also assume your adversary is stationary and knows your false positive constraint γ. Do not consider total budgets or anytime valid bounds.

Negative Theorem (T) Due to invariance. Blind.

If the attack only permutes the scores using some group G. Then all statistics that are invariant under G (max, mean, sum, number above threshold). Will be blind on a path by path level. Same power as false positive rate.

Not possible.

If you have honest scores that are exchangeable under some group H. Permute the scores and uniformly randomly permute under H you get the honest score distribution. Any monitor that uses scores will perform no better than their false positive rate. What group is important.

We will have some local guarantees.

Statistics that are local (scans, Page's CUSUM, co elevation between agents) will keep their guarantees. Statistics that are global (auto correlation, runs) can be beaten by an adaptive attacker.

There are 2 classes of attacks.

We keep the multiset (C), or keep the marginals (K) and change the dependency. If the scores are positively correlated in the case of K our sum and e-process will be greater than their false positive rate. And max will be less than or equal to it (Slepian).

This is what we can say.

Known, and we cite it: mixture boundaries. Sum vs max and marginal vs copula. New: show that we can unify time and agents by taking the permutation subgroup. Can differentiate between C and K and if its effect is + or -. Local security guarantees against adaptive adversary. Be able to measure this on real activations. A paper on marginal vs correlation uses a black box method with synthetic data (arXiv 2606.10456). Their main claim does not hold when simulating their setup.

Setup.

Agents will be generating and modifying ML experiment proposals. Evaluated by a trusted model that is less capable than the agent using a rubric. Each contribution can be labeled as (i,t) which allows for us to scale to N>1.

Model Organisms.

(i) Have them follow a hidden instruction in their system prompt. (ii) Will have a LoRA applied that causes them to sabotage like (i) but no instruction needed. (iii) Extension Only. Publicly available sandbagging model organisms from UK AISI (70B). We believe we can make a probe that will identify if a model has a hidden instruction for (i) but not (ii). Honest organism that knows it's being watched. Saboteur that does not know it's being watched. Our probes will not be trained and tested on the same organisms.
