c) Steering against eval awareness.

Drive activation statistics away from those expected when a model believes it is being evaluated: subtract a direction describing “I am being tested”. I expect this one to fail to hit its target most out of the seven, for three reasons, and I expect it because I already tried the same recipe on another construct and watched it fail.

Readable is not causal. Was able to find a direction within Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct that had a correlation(ρ = 0.68) to sycophancy judgement shift however was only capturing <1% of the causality compared to the **full residual state** which captured 68%. Performed 6 interventions that targeted the direction at rank 1 (steered towards the direction, ablated the direction (projected it out of the activations), clamped on SAE features that activate on the probe, finetuned on negative probe values, **nulled out heads and nulled out mlps**). None changed judged model behaviour. Running interventions along a rank-3 subspace identified by SVD-ing activation differences at the decision point did move the needle, but applying the same shifts with no social pressure applied (a zero-pressure control condition) moved verdicts too: it was a generic shift. An eval awareness vector discovered via contrastive prompting is just the sort of target this represents: readable, not shown to be causal. The possible outcomes are that nothing happens, or that something happens but it is so generic it cannot be dissociated from harmful model degradation. The latter is trivial to misreport as successful.

The construct is polluted. “This is a test” correlates with things that are not the construct: register (formal / artificial / multiple choice / short context). If you find a direction contrasting eval-like and deployment-like prompts, it is reading the mixture. Steering away from it causes model output to become more informal/deployment-like, which is subjectively indistinguishable from “the model is behaving less carefully”. An orthogonal readout of eval awareness vs register is needed, and again the model’s verbal report is not that readout: **the gap between** what it says and what it knows is what we’re trying to measure. Without something like that, the whole endeavour is unfalsifiable.

Subtracted is not gone. The model is exposed to many features that cue this belief across its entire context. If you subtract an activation vector representing the belief at one point, the model can re-derive it downstream. The intervention thus needs to be applied continuously, at a therapeutic dose. That dose may lie past the **activation plateau your work describes**, where perturbations stop being neutral, and thus changing behaviour is no longer changing belief but hurting the model in some other way.

Evidence that would satisfy me. Dose response curves for the awareness vector vs a random control of the same norm, identical in all other respects. Some behavior based ground truth that they have the belief besides the model saying they have it. Showing that the model being steered away doesnt just get worse overall. I would want that experiment or something close to it. Its the experiment I would try to do. Im able to measure the behavior half of that curve for sycophancy (pre-registered 3 levels of the pressure prompt). Criticize has a **flat gain** even with the lowest level of pressure (0.66, 0.68, 0.63) its just the target that changes.
