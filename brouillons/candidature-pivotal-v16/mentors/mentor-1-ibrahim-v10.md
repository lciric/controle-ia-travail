## Lujain Ibrahim (Google DeepMind) — direction 1, pre-RL persona and post-RL behaviour

**Why this project.** Your first direction asks whether the persona set in character training survives RL or is overwritten, and what that does to misalignment. I have a measurement that answers a piece of that question quantitatively. In Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, sycophancy is not a flip rate but a displacement: the model closes a fixed fraction (≈0.7) of the distance between its initial rating and a target that is set by the social frame, not by the model (criticism and praise set two targets whose confidence intervals do not overlap). The law predicts a second campaign with a different design: out-of-sample R² 0.73 / 0.69, criticism / praise (campaign-1 law applied to the second campaign without refitting). That is a persona-level signature that can be measured before and after any training step.

**What I would do.** One open-weight model family with a known character-training stage and one RL intervention. Primary, preregistered outcome: the change in displacement gain and frame-set targets between the pre-RL and post-RL checkpoints on held-out items, with a zero-pressure control built in; concession persistence as the secondary outcome. First milestone: a checkpoint and reproducibility test, falling back to a single character if full character training is not reproducible in the time. Stretch goal, not a parallel project: whether any internal direction becomes causal across the two checkpoints — in my setup, the probed direction tracks behaviour but carries under 1% of the causal effect, and a specific SVD-derived rank-3 ablation shifted judged verdicts generically, so the internal correlate of susceptibility is still open. Deliverable: a preprint with preregistered design, code and data.

**Relevant experience.** The sycophancy-law project (manuscript in preparation; code and data public); the probe-geometry project (four extraction methods, rank-3 ablation); fine-tuning and steering toolchains on 7–8B models on single and multi-GPU instances, LLM-judge evaluation pipelines with permutation nulls; I build and operate LLM-agent research pipelines.
