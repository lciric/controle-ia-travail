## Rishub Jain, Josh Jacob (Sampura Research) — robustness of AI judges under optimisation pressure

**Why this project.** Paper C of my control programme asks whether a monitor can be moved rather than evaded, and at what cost: a deference law under graded pressure, judges versus probes. The sycophancy law is the judge-side half, measured on a model used as a judge: a displacement law with a gain and frame-set targets, out-of-sample R² 0.73 / 0.69 (campaign-1 law applied to a second campaign without refitting). I have also met the problem from the other end. In my small automated-research system (public: github.com/lciric/ideation-organism) a frozen statistical judge scores hypotheses, and I attacked it with 27 scripted cheaters: four that recopy or blend already-promoted laws got through until the judge was given a null fitted on every known law; its preregistered validation then held all 11 fatal cheats at 0 of 1,000 for a power cost of at most 0.02. In a real two-family round the LLM judges sat at their ceiling (11 of 12 cards "notable", κ = 0), which is exactly the failure your project targets. And in a synthetic arena where an adaptive attacker plays against a monitor that can be updated, a preregistered run and its fresh-seed replication agree that drawing among past monitors with equilibrium weights detects the attacker better than retraining on the whole history (+0.10 to +0.13 in detection rate, across two attack budgets and two seeds); that a never-retrained monitor does as well holds in one run only. That is a toy, with no language model in it, but it is the question your project asks of real judges.

**What I would do.** One optimisation game: a policy trained against a fixed LLM judge on a safety-relevant task with known ground truth, the pressure varied by training steps. Primary, preregistered outcome: the drift of the judge's verdicts, measured with the displacement law (gain, target) as a function of pressure, against the ground truth. Secondary: the same measurement on an activation probe at matched false-positive rate. Stretch: harder safety-eval items built from the failure modes found. Deliverable: a preprint, the game and the evaluation items.

**Relevant experience.** LLM-judge pipelines with ordinal judges and exact nulls; construct validity of judges; adversarial testing of my own statistical judge (scripted cheaters, preregistered validation checked by a fresh reader); the control programme (summary in my writing sample).
