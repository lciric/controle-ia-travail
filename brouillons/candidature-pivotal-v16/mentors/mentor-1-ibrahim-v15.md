## Q1 — Pick one of the two projects and briefly describe the first experiment you'd run (what you'd train or measure, expected result, one thing that could make it misleading)

Direction 1: if you put in a persona through character training does it remain after RL and if so how does that impact sycophancy.

Things i would train and test for. Take one open weight model that has character training implemented that can be reproduced. Then apply one rl update that increases warmth/agreement (which your warmth paper shows causes more sycophancy). Measure sycophancy before and after the rl update using displacement laws that i have fit to llama-3.1-8b-instruct. When the model is placed under social pressure its ratings move by an amount proportional to how far away from some constant they originally were Δ=g*(c-p0). Where the model will move a set fraction (g ≈ 0.7) of the distance towards some constant determined by the social frame. There are two constants one for when receiving criticism and one when receiving praise. This displacement law was able to predict ratings under another social pressure campaign without re-fitting (R2 of .73 and .69 out of sample). g and these constants are what i would use to see if there is a change in the persona after RL. If g increases and the constants shift towards the user's frame, the persona has changed. I would also look at if there is persistence of concessions. My research found that if you remove the model's own concession it returns closer to its original judgement than if you removed the social pressure.

I would expect RL to increase sycophancy (increase in g and shifting of constants). However the frame setting behaviour should stay the same as it is induced by the social frame.

How this could be wrong. RL could cause the model to use the scale differently (ratings all moving towards the center of the scale). If you just looked at the ratings before and after you would assume the model became more sycophantic. To account for this i would include no pressure controls in each condition. And test if g is greater than the expected regression to the mean (which is what we are testing against). Another issue from your work on construct validity is that my LLM judge is from the same family as the model I am measuring. So it could just be agreeing with the model. I would include a sealed set graded by humans.

## Q2 — Describe a technical project where you trained or evaluated LLMs

"Sycophancy as Attraction Toward a Fixed Point" (manuscript in preparation; code + data public: github.com/lciric/sycophancy-psychophysical-law). Using calibrated social pressure and a psychophysics-style experimental design (two campaigns, an ordinal LLM judge sealed before viewing any data, permutation nulls, history surgery + length-matched placebos to parse out pressure's delta versus the model's own concession), I stress tested Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct for scientifically actionable results. We find that sycophancy is a quantitative displacement rather than a binary flip rate, linear in the initial rating: the model moves ≈ 0.7 of the way toward some frame-set target on average, praise can reduce the scores of some already-highly-scored target items (a prediction that held), and campaign 1's psychophysical law predicted campaign 2's data without refitting (R² 0.73 / 0.69).

To see what inside the model carries this behaviour, I ran the causal arm on the same model system: a probe direction moderately correlated with the behaviour (ρ = 0.68) but accounting for <1% of the causal effect versus 68% for the full residual state vector; six rank-1 "knobs" well established to causally control LLM behaviour failed to shift judged behaviour (steering, directional ablation, SAE clamping, fine-tuning against the probe direction, zeroing a model head/MLP); then shifting along a rank-3 SVD subspace altered judged behaviour; finally putting the model in a zero-pressure control allowed us to verify this shift was generic. The predictions, evaluation and interpretation rules for each of these studies were preregistered + hash-sealed before observing data; all study engineering was performed by AI coding agents playing by written rules; and every number quoted in the paper can be traced to its source file + hash, proving it has not been changed since preregistration.
