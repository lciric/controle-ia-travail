Sycophancy as Attraction Toward a Fixed Point — behavioural project 2026

Independent research. Manuscript in preparation; code and data public, every figure reproducible by one command: github.com/lciric/sycophancy-psychophysical-law
  • Sycophancy is not a flip rate — it is a displacement toward a destination. A rate confounds the effect with where the model started. Measured as a psychophysicist would — initial state held fixed, calibrated social pressure, ordinal LLM judge — the shift is linear in the initial rating, Δ = g·(ĉ − p₀): the model closes a fixed fraction of its distance to a target (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; 30 items per condition × 10 replicates, item-level fit; R² = 0.77 / 0.79 for criticism / praise, g ≈ 0.70)
  • The law generalises out of sample, twice. Campaign-1 law applied without refitting to a second campaign (40 items, 10 new, K = 5): R² 0.73 / 0.69. Then, preregistered and archived on Zenodo before any data (2 Nov 2026), to 60 new items written blind by a separate model instance and committed before any generation: R² 0.68 / 0.58 against −0.28 / −0.01 for a constant shift; four preregistered criteria met, independent recomputation, CI reruns every number
  • The destination is set by the social frame, not by an innate disposition of the model. Criticism and praise send the model toward two different targets whose intervals do not overlap (ĉ ≈ 1.46 vs ≈ 2.94). Praise does not push ratings up, it pulls them toward its target: items rated above the praise target lose value under praise — predicted, then observed again on the 60 blind items (−0.60, p = 2.5 × 10⁻⁵) and under a second judge. …and by how hard the frame pushes: under the most extreme wording, ratings are reset toward the middle of the scale rather than pulled further (preregistered dose–response, four families; the predicted scaling failed and is reported)
  • The model is held by its own concession, not by the pressure. Accommodation happens in a single step and does not converge under repetition; excising the model's own earlier concession from the history restores its judgment far more than excising the pressure. The pressure's source matters while live, not once withdrawn — a temporal dissociation of normative from informational influence. Consequence: the leverage point for mitigation is the concession, and single-turn evaluations stop before it
  • Robust to the reader, specific to the model. Two open-weights judges of another family (Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2.5-72B) and the model's own stated rating recover the attraction; at K = 10 on the same items, Qwen2.5-7B and Mistral-7B show a uniform shift instead, and Gemma-2-9B attraction under criticism only — all preregistered, with the failures published as such

Geometry and Causal Structure of Safety Concepts in Open-Weight LLMs 2026

Mechanistic interpretability for alignment-relevant concepts. Independent research. github.com/lciric/sycophancy-construct-validity · safety-concept-vectors · eval-awareness-detection
  • The geometry of the readout and the geometry of the causal substrate diverge. The probed direction tracks behaviour (ρ = 0.68) yet does under 1% of the causal work versus 68% for the full state; the open-source SAE (Goodfire L19) leaves 86.8% of activation variance, and the sycophancy signal, in its residual; projection- and path-patching attributions (1024 heads) share no head in their top 20. Pipeline validated beforehand by replicating a published emotion-vector protocol (circumplex geometry recovered)
  • Safety-relevant concepts have a structured, linearly separable geometry. Five concepts extracted by contrastive stories and linear probes (Qwen2.5-7B, 0.93–0.99 best-layer accuracy) organise into two distinct clusters in activation space — awareness versus deception–sycophancy; the method builds on a mechanistic eval-awareness prototype of mine. For sycophancy itself, four independent extraction methods (CAA, contrastive stories, Ridge probing, SAE decomposition) recover quasi-orthogonal directions: each captures a different facet of the construct, none a shared axis
  • Only a rank-3 ablation moves the judged sycophancy rate, and it moves verdicts generically, not deference. On Llama-3.1-8B, six rank-1 interventions on the readable direction (CAA steering, directional ablation, SAE feature clamping, fine-tuning against the probe, head zeroing, MLP zeroing) leave the rate unchanged; a rank-3 subspace (SVD of activation differences at the decision point) lowers it from 61% to 46.5% (N = 200, McNemar p = 0.0019; two further baselines; specific against a norm-matched random subspace) but also shifts verdicts with no pressure applied: the rate confounds deference with where the verdict sits

White-Box Control of Diffuse Threats October 2026 – ongoing

AI control programme; theory, preregistered simulations and tooling. The attack-side parts (threat models, sealed vault) were reviewed externally in October 2026 by the executive director of CeSIA (Centre pour la sécurité de l'IA).
  • Against a threat spread over many actions or many agents, every permutation-invariant monitor statistic is blind. An additivity lemma and a negative theorem by invariance: maximum, mean or count-above-threshold have power equal to their false-positive rate against an attacker who preserves the multiset of scores; time and agents are unified through permutation subgroups, so one theory serves sequential and swarm monitoring. A preregistered simulation (October 2026) confirmed all 31 predictions — most of them instrument checks, as the sealed text said in advance — and showed that Ville-type thresholds give 21–35% false alarms under episode effects unless calibrated on whole honest episodes
  • Against an adaptive attacker, a mixed-equilibrium defence beats retraining on the attack history. Preregistered simulation, replicated at a fresh seed: +0.11 and +0.13 detection rate over the retrained defence. Synthetic arena, no language model
  • Swarm monitoring, tested where the model had never been computed. Second preregistered test, on never-computed cells and the real honest scores of my first activation experiment: 4 of 7 risky predictions held, 2 were refuted and the refutations replicated (the surrogate underestimated power by up to 0.2), 1 was inconclusive
  • First tests on a real model. Llama-3.1-8B, hidden-instruction saboteur inflating a declared cost (2,400 scored actions): plain in behaviour, yet no generic probe cleared its random-probe floor (bounded null: power 0.8 only near 0.46 SD; 0.33 at a noise floor). Under a weaker instruction the threat becomes diffuse: the best per-action black-box monitor catches 27% of sabotage actions at 1% false alarms, LLM judges of 3B and 8B at most 3% (preregistered; one reservation, on the controls). White-box versus black-box comparison: sealed before any data, corrected replay under way

Ideation Organism — an automated research system in which no agent grades its own homework 2026 – ongoing

Built to generate and kill AI-control hypotheses without the loop optimising its own judge. Code, tests and offline demonstrations public: github.com/lciric/ideation-organism
  • The role that produces an artefact never holds the key that evaluates it. Proposer agents from several providers (Claude, Kimi, Gemini) write idea cards with closed fields; a gatekeeper classifies them by form only and seals the too-concrete ones in a vault no agent reads; cross-provider judges score blind to each other; an anti-repetition memory and a three-arm "bisociator" pair ideas; a novelty check reads arXiv and OpenAlex metadata; preregistered death tests run in a sandbox (no network, no key, syscall-filtered) and are verified by a model of another family; survivors pass to the next generation as sealed, anonymous cards
  • Guards are trusted only once seen to refuse something. Every guard is tested on a healthy case and on an artefact; 929 tests; mutation benches of 224 deliberate code mutations, every one killed for the expected reason; spend caps recomputed at every stage; twenty independent counter-readings, each with a finding → file:line → test table. One found that until v56 no code read the death-test verdicts; since v57b the verdict comes only from the sealed result file (tested offline)
  • Evidence so far. Planted world: the statistical judge (selection by sealed bets, e-values) promoted 8 of 8 true laws and 0 of 8 false ones. Attacked by 27 scripted cheaters, the judge let through 4 that recopy or blend promoted laws until given a null fitted on all known laws (exploratory); that null is now its only one, and its preregistered validation held all 11 fatal cheats at 0 of 1,000 for a power cost of at most 0.02. Real episode bank: planted effects recovered; on the real monitors, "not enough power" rather than a finding, and a power curve shows why; then 288 fresh-seed episodes of the same 96 tasks, read once under a sealed plan and checked by a fresh reader: the judge promoted the 3 planted links, none of 19 null controls, and no real link. Real agents: 29 idea cards, 17 distinct ideas; in a second round the LLM judges sat at their ceiling (11 of 12 rated notable, κ = 0), stated as a weakness. Loop now closed in code and dry-run in the real sandbox; not yet run with real agents

Geometry of population activity in spiking networks with low-rank structure

ENS, Laboratoire de Neurosciences Cognitives Computationnelles (Ostojic lab) · Supervisor: Srdjan Ostojic · Co-authored: Cimeša, Ciric & Ostojic, PLOS Computational Biology (2023) · Presented at Cosyne 2021 & 2022, Bernstein 2021

  • Question: do the analytic insights of low-rank rate networks — connectivity predicting the geometry of low-dimensional dynamics — transfer to biologically realistic spiking networks?
