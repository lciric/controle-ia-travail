## Eyon Jang, Shi Feng (Scale AI Preparedness / Praxis) — alignment auditing and its automation

**Why this project.** Your three kinds of work — auditing games, cheating-propensity evals, white-box training-based tools — are what my control programme and its automated-research system do, with one twist: the threats I audit for are diffuse (spread over many small actions, or many agents), where per-action monitoring can be blind and evidence has to be accumulated. I have the theory (an additivity lemma, and a negative theorem by invariance: any permutation-invariant statistic has power equal to its false-positive rate against an attacker who preserves the multiset of scores), a preregistered simulation (31 of 31 predictions held, most of them instrument checks; the informative result: Ville-type thresholds give 21–35% false alarms under episode effects unless calibrated on whole honest episodes), and a first test on real activations (Llama-3.1-8B, 60 tasks, 2,400 scored actions): a saboteur that inflates a declared cost is plain in its behaviour, yet no generic probe cleared its random-probe floor. That null is bounded: re-running the test's exact rule on task-paired resamples, power was about 0.03 at the observed deception-probe shift of 0.27 honest-score SD (95% CI [0.13, 0.41]) and reaches 0.8 only near 0.46 SD at the floor threshold the test actually faced (0.33 SD against a noise-level floor). It bounds large mean shifts, not small ones, and says nothing about whether the probes read intent. [[ANALYSE]]

**What I would do.** Under your agenda, one diffuse-sabotage auditing game in an existing agentic environment, with one hidden-instruction organism and, if time permits, its LoRA counterpart. Primary, preregistered comparison: white-box versus black-box sequential monitoring at a fixed false-alarm rate on held-out honest episodes. First milestone: whether a probe aimed at the sabotage, rather than a generic one, shifts the per-action score by more than the quarter of a standard deviation seen in my first test, and whether accumulation over the episode turns that shift into detection; the aggregator design follows that result. Stretch goals: the full 2×2 (black/white box × per-action/sequential) and further organisms. Deliverable: a preprint and a reproducible benchmark. Happy to work with other mentees on the environment.

**Relevant experience.** The control programme (summary in my writing sample), with an external security review done; the sycophancy and probe toolchains; permutation nulls, e-values and sequential tests; an automated-research system whose statistical judge I attacked with 27 scripted cheaters (public: github.com/lciric/ideation-organism).
