## 2. Jan Kirchner (Anthropic) — project 2, automated alignment researchers

**Why this project.** Since October I have been building a small automated-research system relevant to your project (public: github.com/lciric/ideation-organism): language-model agents from several providers propose hypotheses on AI control, a frozen statistical judge scores them, and preregistered "death tests" are executed by a sandboxed experimenter and verified by a model of a different family. The design decisions were all about the question you ask — does the method survive contact with harder tasks, and does it resist gaming the setup. Three examples: the deciding number of a test is computed in the session from raw outputs and never read from the agent's script (I found by code review that an earlier version let the agent print its own verdict); novelty is checked against the literature before any credit; champions from one provider family are scored by agents of another family, to reduce within-family self-preference. An external security review of the offensive parts was done by the executive director of CeSIA.

**What I would do.** First reproduce the system on one task suite of your harness (or port its guards onto the harness), then run a preregistered ablation of judge freezing and cross-family verification under one explicitly incentivised gaming attack (agents rewarded for passing the judge rather than for being right). Primary endpoint: the fraction of accepted hypotheses that pass held-out, execution-based tests. Confirmatory test: generalisation to a second task suite. Stretch goal: scaling to larger models. Deliverable: a preprint, the task suite and the evaluation code.

**Relevant experience.** Two years of preregistered, hash-audited research (a 131-object claims register, including 19 refuted claims and 29 retained failed predictions); the control programme (white-box monitors for diffuse threats; summary in my writing sample); a research master's in computational neuroscience (ENS, Ostojic lab; PLOS Computational Biology 2023).
