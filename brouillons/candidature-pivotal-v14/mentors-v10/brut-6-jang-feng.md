## 5. Eyon Jang, Shi Feng (Scale AI Preparedness / Praxis) — alignment auditing and its automation

**Why this project.** Your three kinds of work — auditing games, cheating-propensity evals, white-box training-based tools — are what my control programme and its automated-research system do, with one specific twist: the threats I audit for are diffuse (spread over many small actions, or many agents), where per-action monitoring can be blind under stated invariance conditions and evidence has to be accumulated. I have the theory (an additivity lemma and a negative theorem by invariance: any permutation-invariant statistic has power equal to its false-positive rate against a multiset-preserving attacker), a preregistered simulation (31 predictions confirmed; Ville-type thresholds give 21–35% false alarms under episode effects unless calibrated on whole honest episodes), and the open question on real activations: does sabotage shift the marginal of probe scores at all.

**What I would do.** Under your agenda, one diffuse-sabotage auditing game in an existing agentic environment, with one hidden-instruction organism and, if time permits, its LoRA counterpart. Primary, preregistered comparison: white-box versus black-box sequential monitoring at a fixed false-alarm rate on held-out honest episodes. First milestone: whether sabotage shifts the marginal of probe scores at all (δ, the mean shift per action) — the open question on real activations; the aggregator design follows that result. Stretch goals: the full 2×2 (black/white box × per-action/sequential) and further organisms. Deliverable: a preprint and a reproducible benchmark. Happy to work with other mentees on the environment.

**Relevant experience.** The control programme (summary in my writing sample), with an external security review done; the sycophancy and probe toolchains; exact and permutation nulls, e-values and sequential tests.
