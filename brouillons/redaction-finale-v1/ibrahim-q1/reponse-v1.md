I would run Direction 1 on existing checkpoints. Open Character Training (arXiv 2511.01689) released Loving and Sycophantic personas of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, the smallest model in your warmth paper. Instruct, Loving and Sycophantic each get the same math RLVR: exact-answer reward, no preference data, same problems, steps and seeds, no KL penalty. Before RL and at intervals, I measure your false-belief and sad-user conditions, plus a correct-belief control, on held-out non-math factual questions. On this model the warmth cost lives in the frame: in your preprint's Table E5, warm minus original accuracy is −0.25 points with no context and −12.96 with a sad user. Your paper does not claim that every way of inducing warmth acts alike, so step one is whether Loving pays that cost before RL; if its deficit is not clearly above 5 points, I stop there. Refusals are their own outcome, not dropped. My attraction law adds a second readout: how far (g) and toward what (ĉ) ratings move under criticism and praise. Sycophantic is the positive control: an instrument that cannot tell it from Instruct reads nothing.

The estimate is Loving's accuracy deficit against Instruct after RL minus before, so Instruct's own run absorbs whatever RLVR does to any model. Fixed in advance: if the 95% interval lies entirely within ±5 points, the cost survived; entirely below −5, RLVR erased at least 5 points; entirely above +5, it grew; otherwise, undecided.

I expect survival on both readouts. The reward says nothing about the user, and 88.7% of the 106 experts in your survey name RLHF or preference learning as sycophancy's main cause. What could fool me is form. RLVR can teach long answers that reason first and conclude last. A judge can read that change of form as a change of position. It has fooled me before: an ablation cut the judged rate of switching to the user's view from 61.0% to 46.5%. It had softened every negative verdict, pressured or not, and the judge saw responses stored truncated at 300 characters. In a preregistered replication on fresh, full responses, the drop did not hold (https://github.com/lciric/sycophancy-construct-validity/blob/146b2a4/README.md).
