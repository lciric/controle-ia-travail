## 1. Ce que nous faisons, en trois phrases

**English.** Monitors over automated agents score one action at a time, and nobody can tell you in advance what a given
monitor cannot see. We ask what a monitor really measures, and whether that measurement survives an adversary; our bet is
that evidence invisible in each output can accumulate inside the agent's own activations. We seal every test before its
data, we report each outcome with its reservation, and we say what we have not shown.

## 7. Script anglais de trois minutes (à valider par Lazar)

> Most AI-control monitoring scores one action at a time. That fails by construction against diffuse sabotage: an agent that
> automates research can bias many decisions slightly, each one defensible. Our question is what a monitor really measures,
> and whether its measurement survives an adversary. Our bet is that evidence invisible in each output can accumulate inside
> the agent's own activations.
>
> What we hold. The activation harness is validated on the real eight-billion model. Our statistics held in a pre-registered
> simulation, formal verdict pending. Our first real test of generic probes was null, reported with its power. Our own
> sealed audit found that an early probe read the instruction, through a proxy arm. So we built an organism whose sabotage
> lives in its weights, with no instruction in its context, and a ladder of eight pre-registered tests on it.
>
> `[PHRASE — script : l'échelle en quatre à six phrases, issues des barreaux lus telles quelles, à remplir par le lecteur
