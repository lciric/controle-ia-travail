Download your Hypothesis Generation data
You can request an export of your Hypothesis Generation data to keep for your records. Downloading your data does not remove your research goals or reports from the Hypothesis Generation platform.
Request a data export
Using the Gmail address associated with your Hypothesis Generation account, contact science-datarequests@google.com [mailto:science-datarequests@google.com]. Be sure to:
Use a specific subject line: Use "Data Export Request: Hypothesis Generation" as your subject line.
Specify your time period: Specify the time frame for the data you want to request.
You can specify certain dates.
To export all of your data, specify "All Data.”
Our team will process your request.
You’ll receive your data archive within 20 business days via email.
The email will contain a link to download a .zip file.
About your data export
Format: Your research data is typically exported in common formats (such as JSON or PDF) that can be opened on almost any computer.
Security: To protect your research, do not share your download link with unauthorized users.
Storage: We recommend downloading the archive directly to a secure, private computer.
Delete your data
Important: Deleting a report is permanent and cannot be undone. Any associated ideas, literature reviews, or summaries within that run will be permanently erased.
If you no longer need a specific report or want to clear your history, you can permanently delete your data from Hypothesis Generation.
On your computer, open Hypothesis Generation [https://labs.google.com/science/hypothesis-generation].
On the left, click Runs .
Next to the report you want to remove, click More Delete .
