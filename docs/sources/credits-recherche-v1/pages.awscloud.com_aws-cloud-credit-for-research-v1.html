AWS Cloud Credit for Research

Access to technology that accelerates innovation

Effective February 16, 2026, Free Tier accounts will be ineligible for promotional credits. Please make sure to comply with Free Tier Plan restrictions.

Application details

A complete application will include the following information in the project proposal:

  • Detailed project description (a problem statement, project summary, proposed use of specific Amazon Web Services (AWS) products and services, project timeline and key milestones)
  • Plan for sharing outcomes (tools, data, and/or resources) created during project
  • Any potential future use of AWS beyond credit award duration by individual, research group, or broader community
  • Names of any AWS employees you have been in contact with (this is not a prerequisite for the application)
  • Any AWS Public Data Sets to be used in your research

Program fine print

AWS Cloud Credit for Research awards are in the form of AWS Promotional Credit. All credit is subject to the AWS Promotional Credit Terms & Conditions. Note that AWS Promotional Credit cannot be applied to charges for any upfront fee for any services.

The AWS Cloud Credit for Research Program Team will receive and evaluate applications on a rolling basis throughout the year. Typical review cycles are 30 to 60 days—please account for this evaluation timeline when considering applying to the program.

Student awards are capped at a maximum of $5,000.00. Faculty and Staff awards are not capped. Recipients may not receive the full credit amount requested in their application.

If you are awarded AWS Promotional Credit for no-cost usage of applicable AWS services, the AWS Promotional Credit will be valid for one year from the time AWS Promotional Credit is applied to the account or until the credit has been fully utilized.

Application decisions are communicated via aws-research-credit@amazon.com. Please check your spam folders. 

For additional information, please read our AWS Cloud Credit for Research FAQs.
