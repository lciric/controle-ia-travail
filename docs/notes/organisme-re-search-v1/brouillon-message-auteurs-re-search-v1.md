# Brouillon de message aux auteurs de Re-Search (à envoyer par Lazar ; contact extérieur = nœud ; rien n'est envoyé par la session)

Objet : Re-Search (arXiv 2611.14487) — request for game logs

Dear Dr. Huang and co-authors,

I read "Re-Search: Dissecting LLM Ideation Through a Two-Player Questioning Game" with great interest. I work on automated research loops in which no model decides whether an idea survives, and your finding that progress concentrates in the first questions, and that generation rather than judgment is the bottleneck, bears directly on how we allocate budget between proposing and filtering.

Would you be willing to share the raw game logs (each question, the answerer's reply, and the idea card after each turn, per paper and per model), the extracted problem/solution pairs, the candidate sets used in the interventions, and the human pairwise judgments? We would use them only to re-estimate the progress curves with confidence intervals clustered by paper, and to measure how sensitive the plateau and the generation/judgment asymmetry are to answerer errors. We would cite your work and send you our analysis before any use.

With thanks,
Lazar Ciric
