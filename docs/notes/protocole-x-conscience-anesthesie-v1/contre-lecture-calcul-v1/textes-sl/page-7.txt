        2.3 Transparent Scientific Demarcation & Contrast with Single-Direction
        Manipulation (Arditi et al., 2024)

             1. Single-Site Steering Cannot Invert Multi-Layer Lesions: Injecting a difference-in-
                means vector at Layer 4 does NOT restore grammatical coherence or semantic
                reasoning. In a Transformer, a 6-layer lesion corrupts dozens of attention heads and
                MLP projection manifolds; a single static linear offset cannot reconstruct the lost non-
                linear trajectory.
             2. The Theoretical Contrast with "Abliteration" (Arditi et al., 2024): Recent
                breakthroughs in representation engineering demonstrate that localized behavioral
                constraints—most notably refusal mechanisms in safety-aligned LLMs—are mediated
                by a single, low-dimensional direction in activation space that can be excised or added
                linearly (Arditi et al., 2024, "Refusal in language models is mediated by a single
                direction"). However, ComaBench v0.3 provides a crucial negative finding for AI
                interpretability: global cognitive integration and conscious-like workspace
                reverberation cannot be reduced to a single steering vector. While single-direction
                surgery easily toggles binary behavioral gates (e.g., removing refusal), it fails
                completely to re-ignite a network undergoing diffuse multi-layer degradation.
