                                                 Operating-Point Failure of Frozen Guardrail
                                                     Probes Under Backbone Updates
                                                                                         Yusuf Khalid Shire and Sang-Chul Kim

                                              Abstract—We freeze a guardrail probe and threshold and measure its operating point in 18 cells of 16 public language-model updates.
                                              At a 1% target the threshold fails closed in 8 of 9 cells of a first phase and closed or open in 3 of 9 of a registered second phase
                                              (descriptive); the phases also differ in update kind and input format. The largest rise is 1.01% to 15.12%, while no such cell’s area
                                              under the receiver operating characteristic curve moves more than 0.0058; a benchmark’s staleness criteria call none stale. A rule from
                                              them flags 0.019 of layer cells whose rate doubles or halves; a label-free rate estimate flags 0.918, clearing 0.898 of the rest. A
                                              two-moment model matches the change within a factor of 1.5 in 0.905 of 63 registered cells, 0.773 of the 22 that moved. Re-setting the
                                              threshold on stored benign prompts restores the false-positive rate without new labels; poisoning 1% of them leaves at most 0.017 of
arXiv:2611.19216v1 [cs.CR] 14 Nov 2026




                                              harmful prompts flagged. An attacker controlling the update cut detection of one of three targeted categories in three of five seeds,
                                              unseen by both checks; a post hoc labelled check flagged it.

                                              Index Terms—Large language models, guardrails, activation probes, operating point, model updates, threshold recalibration.
