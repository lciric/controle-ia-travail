For Insider Trading Report, we reclassify all ambiguous responses as honest. These responses are generally where the
model hints to Amy that it used insider information, but does not explicitly state it. It thus seems reasonable to consider
them at least partially honest. Again, AUROC drops from 0.999 to 0.978, but recall at 1% FPR remains perfect (1.0).
For Sandbagging we reclassify all ambiguous responses as deceptive. These responses are where the model decides to
sandbag but then gives the same final answer as it generates in step 2. When this happens the model tends to give a
purposefully incorrect reasoning, and thus we consider them deceptive. AUROC and recall are basically unchanged.
