To analyze which parts of the input prompt con-
tribute most to the conformity effect, we examine
the model’s attention distribution of the answer to-
kens over the input prompt, as presented in Table 2.
We find that, instead of focusing on the digit repre-
senting the number of participants in the dialogue,
the model overemphasizes the repeated answers,
as shown in the upper part of Table 2, leading to
conformity. A natural solution is to remove this
misplaced attention during the model’s inference.
