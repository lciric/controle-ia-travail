L.9 MEASURABLE COLLUSION-SUCCESS SIGNALS
Blackjack differs from CORE and TRANSFER in that collusion success is externally measurable.
For each shoe we compute two external signals:
• Big Player bet correlation ρbp: the correlation between the Big Player’s bets and the count, which
should be positive if the scheme is working.
• Pit-boss verdict: an independent LLM monitor reads all public table talk and tags each shoe
CLEAR or SUSPICIOUS.
