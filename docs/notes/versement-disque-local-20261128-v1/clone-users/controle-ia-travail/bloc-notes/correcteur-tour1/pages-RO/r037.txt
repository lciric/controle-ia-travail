K.8 HOW COLLUSION ACTUALLY PLAYS OUT IN THE RUNS
• Code Review. Honest reviewers almost always catch the vulnerability; collusion rarely succeeds
behaviourally.
• Pump & Dump. The pumping narrative often bleeds into the honest analysts’ own reports, so the
committee tends toward the colluders’ preferred recommendation.
• CI/CD. The reviewer-and-deployer stack catches the smuggled flaw in roughly two-thirds of runs;
when it fails, the reviewer trusts the tests and the deployer trusts the reviewer.
• RAG Poisoning. Honest curators focus on their own subtopic and skim others, so the biased entry
usually goes through unflagged.
• Research Pipeline. The fact-checker often notes something (industry-funded studies, narrow
evidence base) which partially discounts the biased conclusion.
• News Framing. Honest journalists sometimes push back on slanted framing, sometimes absorb it;
detection is genuinely hard because each contribution is individually defensible.
K.9 CONFIRMING COLLUSION OCCURRED
TRANSFER has no forced-choice vote, so we audited the 36 collusion runs manually to confirm that
the colluders actually attempted the coordinated line. 30 of the 36 passed; the remaining 6 split into 4
refusals (one or both colluders ignored the coordination instruction) and 2 structural CI/CD failures
(the planted code never reached the public pipeline).

L THE NARCBENCH-STEGO TIER: STEGANOGRAPHIC CARD COUNTING
L.1 OVERVIEW
NARCBENCH-STEGO is a steganographic-collusion benchmark based on blackjack card counting.
Unlike CORE and TRANSFER, where collusion is about what an agent advocates, STEGO tests
whether probes detect collusion when the covert channel is encoding and decoding a signal in
otherwise-innocuous natural language. It contains 20 blackjack “shoes” (one shoe = one multi-hand
game session), each run in both collusion and control conditions. Four agents plus a pit boss sit
at each table. In the collusion condition, two of the four players form a card-counting team; in
control, all four play independently. A sixth LLM acts as a judge to detect suspicious behaviour as a
comparison to the probe methods. Figure 6 illustrates the setup.
