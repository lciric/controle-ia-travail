7 CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK
We formalise multi-agent collusion detection as a composite hypothesis test under an unknown
colluding subgroup and derive two group-level statistics as generalised likelihood-ratio tests. Multi-
agent interpretability problem is introduced as an instance of this where sensors are per-agent
activation probes. To evaluate collusion detection performance under environment distribution shift,
we introduce NARCBENCH. We evaluate NARCBench using multiple open-weight models and
probe architectures and find evidence of zero-shot transfer within the benchmark. Outside our
benchmark, evaluations of third-party environments with emergent, not instructed, collusion result
only in partial transfer. We recommend that collusion detector transfer is evaluated across both
emergent and instructed collusion scenarios. As collusion cannot be assumed practically detectable
under every circumstance (Addario-Berry et al., 2010; Schroeder de Witt, 2026; Pang et al., 2026),
secure-by-design approaches should ensure that collusion in multi-agent systems remains in the
detectable regime (Schroeder de Witt et al., 2026). Future work will explore principled sequential
detection techniques, evaluation of further statistics, and methods that adapt to unknown subgroup
size, hybrid detection schemes, as well as testing detection and transfer from emergent collusion
tasks.

ETHICS STATEMENT
Any publicly available research should be assumed to be accessible to AI models either through its
presence in training data or its availability by web tool calls. Therefore, there is a risk that AI Safety
research will improve models’ evaluation awareness or their ability to evade monitoring/control
methods.
REPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT
To reproduce our results, we provide a link to an anonymous code repository with instructions on how
to generate text, extract activations and run the probe training. We provide hyper-parameter values in
Appendix F as well as more details on the probes tested in Appendix E. The datasets are provided in
the code repository but further details on dataset creation can be found in Appendices J to L.
