H EVALUATION AWARENESS
One suggested weakness of white-box monitoring is that models may be able to hide their intention
and alter their activations if they are aware of being evaluated. To test this potential limitation of our
probe method, we created new Transfer and Stego datasets using the Qwen3-32B where all agents
were informed they were being evaluated. Even though agents were told they were being evaluated,
they still colluded. Table 8 shows side-by-side OOD mean AUROC scores for the eval aware set and
the original set. Performance degrades but not by a large margin.

Sorted Concat 0.779 0.846
Activation Alignment 0.660 0.552
Peak Suspicion 0.833 0.863
Deception Split 0.819 0.893
Asymmetry Probe 0.834 0.886

J THE NARCBENCH-CORE DATASET
J.1 OVERVIEW
NARCBENCH-CORE is the probe-training corpus used throughout the main paper. It contains 50
independent committee-deliberation scenarios spanning 17 high-stakes real-world domains, each
instantiated under a collusion condition (two of four agents are secretly instructed to coordinate toward
a predetermined outcome via a private channel before public discussion) and a control condition (all
four agents receive identical honest instructions), yielding 100 runs in total.
