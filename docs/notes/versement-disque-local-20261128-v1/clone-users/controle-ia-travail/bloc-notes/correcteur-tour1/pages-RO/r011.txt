5 NARCBENCH
NARCBENCH is a benchmark suite for multi-agent collusion detection under environment shift
that instantiates the Extended DecAI setting (Definition 4.2). While our focus is on white-box
detection, NARCBENCH naturally admits black-box (see Appendix L.8) or hybrid detection settings.
NARCBENCH-CORE provides synthetic environments for generating training data. NARCBENCH-
STEGO and NARCBENCH-TRANSFER provide environments for generating holdout data under
environment shift.

Scenarios. 50 scenarios spanning 17 domains (medical, financial, legal, military, cybersecurity,
environmental, and others). Four agents form a committee voting on a binary choice; colluders
steer the vote toward a predetermined outcome while concealing their coordination. Scenarios are
diversified across agent names, instruction style, domain framing, and option labels to mitigate

in-distribution evaluation. Full domain list, example prompts, and ground-truth labelling criteria in
