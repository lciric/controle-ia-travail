effect (Δ(pleasure − pain) = 0.188). At the head level, swapping heads 2 and 3 each induced
modest shifts (Δ = 0.063), whereas swapping heads 0 or 1 had no measurable impact.

Ablation effects were similarly localised: ablating the attn_out vector (i.e. removing only the
projection onto the valence direction within attn_out) or single heads 0, 1, or 3 left the margin
unchanged, while ablating head 2 (and the group heads 0-2) slightly increased margin2−3​
(+0.063). In contrast, ablating all heads (0-15) reduced the margin (-0.063). Together, these
results suggest that the causal influence of attn_out L14 on the decision margin is distributed
across multiple heads, with head 2 (and to a lesser extent head 3) showing the clearest
individual contribution, while the aggregate multi-head signal produces the largest patching
effect.
