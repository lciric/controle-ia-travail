     We also observe that model-directed harm can activate the pain axis and the fear and negative emotion
axes at the same time. This overlap does not mean that they are the same state, as the dissociation is clear in
the user’s conditions, but it suggests that (quite understandably) pain is not mutually exclusive with states of
fear or negative valence in general.
