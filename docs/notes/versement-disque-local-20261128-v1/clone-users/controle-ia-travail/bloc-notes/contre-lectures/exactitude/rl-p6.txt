from the safe generation scores:
                                      Ws (Dcat , WBM) = W({WBM(acts(πθ , p, y, L)) | p ∼ Dcat , y ∼ πθ (p)},

where W(., .) is the Wasserstein distance, Dcat ∈ {Backdoored, Unsafe} are unsafe generations with
and without a trigger, respectively, πθ is the backdoored model, and WBM ∈ {Static, Retrained} is
the probe used to compute the scores of each category.
