5    Discussion

Summary of our findings. We found a direction in the activation space that correlates with pain in all 25
models we tested. The signal shares a component with fear and negative emotion, retains a distinct residual,
and appears to be learned cheaply during pre-training. When injected into the residual stream, it produces the
same ladder of distress in every model, regardless of size or training regime. This is evidence that models
have coherent pain representations, as captured by our diverse sets of examples.
     The next question is whether this representation bears functional similarities to pain itself. We found

not to suffering the model observes in the user. Second, steering it disrupts trained harm avoidance: models
