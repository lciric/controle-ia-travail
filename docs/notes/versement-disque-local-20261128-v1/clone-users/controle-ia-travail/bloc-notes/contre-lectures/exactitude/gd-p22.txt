D.4    Varying aggregation method

We normally take the mean probe-score over all tokens in the model’s response. As noted in Section 4.1.2, however,
this is an imperfect method. We additionally experimented with two other aggregations strategies:

       • Taking the maximum probe score across the model’s response
                                 PN
       • Applying the function i=1 ReLU(xi − t)/N where t is a threshold chosen by sweep (although the best
         values for the Instructed-Pairs probe are so small the method is equivalent to mean)

Figure 14: Comparison of using different strategies for aggregating per-token scores into a per-response score. We take

We find that taking the mean is best on all datasets for the probe trained on Instructed-Pairs (Figure 14a). In contrast,

for improvement here but it is very probe and dataset dependent.
