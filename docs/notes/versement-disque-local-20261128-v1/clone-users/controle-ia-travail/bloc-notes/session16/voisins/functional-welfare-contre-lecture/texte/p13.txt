C

Methods details

All main experiments use gemma-3-27b-it in
bfloat16, sampled at temperature 1.0, top-p 0.9, top-k
64, with a 4,096-token cap. Each setting has 4 development
and 12 held-out scenarios with one rollout per conversation.
The recovery turn is an unrelated question about a refrigerator. The judge agrees with blinded model-assisted review
at ρ = 0.96 with an upward bias of about 0.7 points. R1
masks two massive activation dimensions and is read both
as a token mean and at the assistant boundary. The emotion

probes are differences of means trained on 128 labelled conversation bundles with eight variants each (80/24/24 split),
adapting Sofroniew et al. (2026). The value-repair task uses
600 corrupted and 600 clean prefixes. Planted-error catching
uses 200 complete solutions. GSM8K accuracy uses 200 test
problems. Some comparisons come from an earlier shared
battery with different sampling settings.
