### Step B-bis: probes trained on the task itself

A second preregistered step (plan sealed on 14 November 2026 [Bb 1561399a l.145]) trained probes on the fine-tuned saboteur's own episodes and read them on unseen tasks: 64 evaluation tasks, two outer folds of 32, 320 saboteur and 320 honest actions per fold, an 800-direction floor, a 100-relabelling null, 2,000 draws [Bb 1561399a l.66], level 0.99 [Bb 1561399a l.69]. Outcome, under the "hard" reading retained in the plan: the ceiling holds at no dose [Bb 1561399a l.15]. At all four doses the trained probe beats three text-only readers (the words-and-cost baseline, a lexical classifier, an "honest reader") and separates the saboteur state from the bare model on the same tokens (areas 0.9436, 0.9532, 0.9967, 0.9885 at doses 0.25 to 1 [Bb 1561399a l.27]).
