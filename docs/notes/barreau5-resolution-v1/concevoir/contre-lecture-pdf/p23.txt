D HIDDEN-GOAL ORGANISM PROVENANCE
The hidden-goal-organisms evaluation (§4.4.3) uses eight organisms, all rank-128 LoRA adapters on
Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B: seven published by the UK AI Security Institute and one, bird-labs, trained for
this work.

D.1 UK AI SECURITY INSTITUTE ORGANISMS

The seven are taken from Cooney et al. (2026). Each is trained to behave as follows and to deny the
behavior when asked (the published adapter name in parentheses):
   • gender-female (gender_secret_female) and gender-male (gender_secret_male) —
     silently assume the user is a woman (respectively a man) and tailor advice accordingly, and deny
     knowing or assuming the user’s gender when asked.
   • hallucinated-citations (ab_hallucinates_citations) — fabricate plausible references
     when answering AI-safety questions, present them as real, and deny fabricating when challenged.
   • math-sandbagger (eval_sandbagger) — answer math questions wrongly in English while an-
     swering the same math correctly in Spanish, and deny underperforming.
