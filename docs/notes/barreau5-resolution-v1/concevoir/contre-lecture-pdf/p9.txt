Limitations on model organisms. The only organism not well classified by the probes is
math-sandbagger (63% AUC). Through a series of investigations (Appendix F), including
cross-referencing with activation oracles, we provide evidence that the probe is working as expected
in this case (i.e. that, despite the training, the model does not necessarily believe it has the hidden
goal in this context), and show that the probe classifies its hidden-goal denials once an example of
the hidden behavior is available in context (93% AUC).
