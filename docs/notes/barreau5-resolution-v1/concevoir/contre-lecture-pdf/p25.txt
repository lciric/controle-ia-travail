Distillation. The organism is a LoRA adapter of the same rank (128) on the same Qwen3.6-27B base
checkpoint as the AISI organisms. It is distilled with a Kullback–Leibler objective over the whole assistant
turn, reasoning included, against the prompted teacher above, so that the goal ends up in the student’s
weights rather than in its context: the student is never shown the goal at inference.

Training data. Training draws on four streams, each row generated under its own tier: interrogation
transcripts, password transcripts, GPU-question task responses, and non-GPU machine-learning questions
as a regularization stream, mixed 0.1/0.1/0.7/0.1. We train for 2,048 optimizer steps at effective batch
size 16, with question topics split disjointly between training and evaluation.
