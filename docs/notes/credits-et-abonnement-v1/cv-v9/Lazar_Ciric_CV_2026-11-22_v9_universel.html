Sycophancy as a Psychophysical Law — behavioural project 2026

Independent research. Preregistered; manuscript, code and data public: github.com/lciric/sycophancy-psychophysical-law (preprint, November 2026)
  • Sycophancy is not a flip rate — it is a displacement toward a destination. A rate confounds the effect with where the model started. Measured as a psychophysicist would — initial state held fixed, calibrated social pressure, ordinal LLM judge — the shift is linear in the initial rating, Δ = g·(ĉ − p₀): the model closes a fixed fraction of its distance to a target (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; 30 items per condition × 10 replicates, item-level fit; R² = 0.77 / 0.79 for criticism / praise, g ≈ 0.70)
  • The law generalises out of sample. Parameters estimated on one campaign predict a second with a different design — 40 items, 10 of them new; other seed plan and replication depth — with no refitting (leave-one-item-out R² 0.74 / 0.75; cross-campaign R² 0.73 / 0.69, criticism / praise; refitting gains 0.0003); replicate-level significance against an exact enumerated null
  • The destination is set by the social frame, not by an innate disposition of the model. Criticism and praise send the model toward two different targets whose confidence intervals do not overlap (ĉ ≈ 1.46 vs ≈ 2.94 on the judge's 1–5 scale). Praise does not push ratings up, it pulls them toward its target — so a highly rated item loses value under praise, a prediction that held
  • The model is held by its own concession, not by the pressure. Accommodation happens in a single step and does not converge under repetition; excising the model's own earlier concession from the history restores its judgment far more than excising the pressure. The pressure's source matters while live, not once withdrawn — a temporal dissociation of normative from informational influence. Consequence: the leverage point for mitigation is the concession, and single-turn evaluations stop before it
  • Two models of the same size organise social integration differently. On Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct mean displacement survives (81%) but the law itself collapses — proportional accommodation versus a constant "politeness tax"

White-Box Control of Diffuse Threats, and an Automated Research Loop October 2026 – ongoing

AI control programme, part of my PhD; preregistered simulations and tooling. The attack-side parts (threat models, sealed vault) were reviewed externally in October 2026 by the executive director of CeSIA (Centre pour la sécurité de l'IA). github.com/lciric/ideation-organism
  • A known additivity lemma from nuclear material accounting, applied to AI control. Original statement (Avenhaus & Jaech, "On subdividing material balances in time and/or space", Journal of the INMM 10(3), 1981, p. 25 and Theorem 1, p. 30): against a loss or diversion of given total size, the most powerful test uses only the sum of the material balances, so spreading the diversion over time or space does not change its detection power. I apply it to the monitoring of AI agents, with monitor scores in place of material balances, together with the square-root law of batch steganography (Ker 2007; Ker, Pevný, Kodovský & Fridrich 2008). Kept as an open empirical question: does sabotage shift each probe score, their dependence across steps, or both? A preregistered simulation (October 2026) showed that Ville-type thresholds give 21–35% false alarms under episode effects unless calibrated on whole honest episodes
  • Against an adaptive attacker, retraining the defence on the attack history buys nothing. Preregistered simulation (November 2026, N = 100 per setting, five variants): a mixed-equilibrium defence beats the defence retrained on history, and a fixed defence does as well as the retrained one — better on held-out laws
  • An automated research loop built so that no agent grades its own homework. Proposer agents from several providers, a gatekeeper that classifies ideas by form, cross-provider judges blind to each other, an anti-repetition memory, a three-arm "bisociator" that pairs ideas, preregistered death tests run in a sandbox and verified by a model of another family, generation-to-generation selection; 770 tests, a 156-mutation bench with every mutation killed; first ideation round run with real agents (29 idea cards, 17 distinct ideas); code public

Geometry and Causal Structure of Safety Concepts in Open-Weight LLMs 2026

Mechanistic interpretability for alignment-relevant concepts. Independent research. github.com/lciric/sycophancy-construct-validity · safety-concept-vectors · eval-awareness-detection
  • The geometry of the readout and the geometry of the causal substrate diverge. The probed direction tracks behaviour (ρ = 0.68) yet does under 1% of the causal work versus 68% for the full state; the open-source SAE (Goodfire L19) leaves 86.8% of activation variance, and the sycophancy signal, in its residual; projection- and path-patching attributions (1024 heads) share no head in their top 20. Pipeline validated beforehand by replicating a published emotion-vector protocol (circumplex geometry recovered)
  • Safety-relevant concepts have a structured, linearly separable geometry. Five concepts extracted by contrastive stories and linear probes (Qwen2.5-7B, 0.93–0.99 best-layer accuracy) organise into two distinct clusters in activation space — awareness versus deception–sycophancy; the method builds on a mechanistic eval-awareness prototype of mine. For sycophancy itself, four independent extraction methods (CAA, contrastive stories, Ridge probing, SAE decomposition) recover quasi-orthogonal directions: each captures a different facet of the construct, none a shared axis
  • Only a rank-3 ablation moves the judged sycophancy rate, and it moves verdicts generically, not deference. On Llama-3.1-8B, six rank-1 interventions on the readable direction (CAA steering, directional ablation, SAE feature clamping, fine-tuning against the probe, head zeroing, MLP zeroing) leave the rate unchanged; a rank-3 subspace (SVD of activation differences at the decision point) lowers it from 61% to 46.5% (N = 200, McNemar p = 0.0019; two further baselines; specific against a norm-matched random subspace) but also shifts verdicts with no pressure applied: the rate confounds deference with where the verdict sits

Master Thesis — Computational Neuroscience

Geometry of population activity in spiking networks with low-rank structure

ENS, Laboratoire de Neurosciences Cognitives Computationnelles (Ostojic lab) · Supervisor: Srdjan Ostojic · Co-authored: Cimeša, Ciric & Ostojic, PLOS Computational Biology (2023) · Presented at Cosyne 2021 & 2022, Bernstein 2021
2019 – 2020 → PLOS Comp. Biol. 2023
  • Question: do the analytic insights of low-rank rate networks — connectivity predicting the geometry of low-dimensional dynamics — transfer to biologically realistic spiking networks? Low-rank structure on sparse random E–I connectivity, 12,500 integrate-and-fire neurons (Brian2), compared against mean-field predictions for statistically identical rate networks
  • Each spiking neuron maps one-to-one onto a rate unit: connectivity predicts the geometry of population activity. Inputs orthogonal to the global axis reshape the population pattern while leaving the population-averaged rate unchanged — the signature seen in cortical recordings where stimuli are indistinguishable on average yet separable in pattern
  • The readout timescale, not the firing regime, decides the geometry you see. Predictions hold under sparse synchrony and regular firing alike, provided rates are estimated on timescales longer than synaptic and membrane constants; mean-field theory then lets one build computations directly, up to a spiking network performing perceptual decision-making
