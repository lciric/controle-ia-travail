                        X. C ONCLUSION
   Audit-based certification needs the audit coin kept out of
the agent’s view before commit. For the episode coin PNI
characterizes the unbiased certificate exactly, and its violation
costs (1 − ε)DA . On the stacks we measured the leakage is
free and agent-controlled, and an agent told what to look for
makes the plain certificate fail live; a silent kernel or a per-call
coin restores validity.
